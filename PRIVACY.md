# URL Grabber & Video Downloader — Privacy Policy

**A Forge.plops.io project. Sponsored by Yrill.com.**

Effective October 30, 2026.

URL Grabber works locally in your browser. The installed Chrome extension is named **Incognito Tab Exporter**. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising trackers, remote code, or separate URL Grabber server. Forge.plops.io and Yrill.com branding does not add data collection to the extension.

## Data the extension processes

The extension processes tab URLs, titles, page headings, accessible video URLs, download settings, and download progress only to provide the features you request. Exports, settings, video mappings, and partial download chunks are stored locally. Completed files are saved on your device. Regular and incognito sessions use separate extension storage behavior.

## Website requests

Video requests go directly from your browser to the existing website hosting the video. Those requests may include credentials already associated with that site. URL Grabber does not collect those credentials, bypass logins or DRM, or send page data to Forge.plops.io, Yrill.com, or another URL Grabber service.

## Sharing and permitted use

Data is not sold or transferred to third parties by the extension, used for unrelated purposes, or used for creditworthiness or lending.

## Your control

Clearing extension/site data or removing the extension can delete locally stored settings and partial chunks. Downloaded files and exports you copied elsewhere remain under your control.
